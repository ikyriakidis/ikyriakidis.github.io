About me

My name is Iraklis Kyriakidis. I’m a programmer, blogger and technologist. I live in Chania, Crete, Greece and work remotely as a Software Engineer — though this website, its content and its opinions are my own.

I write and think about technology, culture, gadgets, diversity, code, the web, where we’re going and where we’ve been. I’m excited about community, finance, entrepreneurship and, above all, the open web.

Background

I hold a bachelor’s degree from the University of Hertfordshire, United Kingdom, and a master’s degree from Stockholm University, Sweden. I now live in Chania, Greece and work remotely as a Software Engineer.

Most of my time goes into back-end services on Microsoft technologies — C#, VB.NET, ASP.NET Core, .NET, Web API — without limiting myself to those. I also work with Kafka, RabbitMQ, Redis, and whatever tool, language or technology fits the use case best. I’ve worked on many websites and services that people still use every day.

An agile advocate, building software with clean architecture, TDD, BDD and DDD.
