What I do

I’m a software engineering manager and backend specialist. Most of my work sits where financial systems meet hard technical constraints — high volumes, strict regulation, and data that has to be right the first time.

Financial crime & compliance

AML, sanctions and watchlist screening, and transaction monitoring. Systems that have to satisfy regulators as well as engineers, where a false negative is a legal problem and a false positive is an operational one.

Payments & transaction processing

Wallets, BNPL, invoicing, loan management and cashless payment flows. Real-time systems handling millions of transactions a day, where staying correct under load is the whole requirement.

Backend & distributed architecture

APIs and data-intensive services in C# and .NET, on SQL Server, with Kafka, RabbitMQ and Redis where the shape of the problem calls for them. Transaction processing at volume, designed to stay correct under load.

Cloud & delivery

Azure and AWS, containerised with Docker and Kubernetes, deployed through CI/CD pipelines. Infrastructure that a team can actually operate, rather than a diagram that only works on the day it was drawn.

Legacy modernization

Taking systems that still earn their keep but have stopped being safe to change, and getting them moving again — incrementally, in production, without a rewrite that never lands.

Engineering leadership

Leading teams, running 1:1s, performance reviews and hiring, owning delivery planning, and sitting on architecture committees. Technical direction across teams, not just within one.

How I work

I build with clean architecture, TDD, BDD and DDD, and I’m an agile advocate — though I care more about whether a team can ship confidently on a Friday than about which ceremony it holds on a Monday.

I work closely with product, engineering and business stakeholders, turning complex requirements into practical technical solutions. In regulated domains that translation is most of the job: the hard part is rarely the code, it’s agreeing precisely what the system is supposed to guarantee.

Based in Greece (EET/EEST), working remotely, after a decade in distributed Nordic and European teams. Comfortable working asynchronously, with overlap into US Eastern mornings.
